About

I build Android apps in Kotlin and have been doing it commercially since 2018. I've worked on e-commerce apps used by millions (Reserved, Sinsay, Big Star) and now on ErgData, Concept2's companion app that connects to rowing machines and bikes over Bluetooth Low Energy.

I care about performance, crash-free rates and code a team can keep changing for years. I'm comfortable being the person the client talks to: on several projects I represented the Android team, ran technical decisions and mentored junior developers. Lately I'm spending a lot of time with Kotlin Multiplatform and AI-assisted development workflows.
