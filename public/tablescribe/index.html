Open threads

Every hunch, kept with its evidence.

Every "I bet…" and "maybe it's…" in your notes becomes an open thread, linked to the sessions and clues behind it. Ten sessions later, it is still there.

No extra writing

Players write notes as usual. Threads are pulled out of what is already there.

Evidence attached

Each thread lists the clues that point to it and who noticed them.

Private stays private

A hunch from a player's private notes is visible only to that player and you.

Spoiler-free for GMs

Rather not be swayed by your players' theories? They stay hidden from you until you switch them on.

Five notebooks. One story.

Where AI comes in

It keeps the records. You tell the story.

Tablescribe uses AI to read your table's notes, connect them and point out what you may have forgotten. The creative part stays with you.

  • Files, links and remembers what your table wrote
  • Reminds you of loose ends and suggests ways back to your plan
  • Never runs your session or makes calls at the table
  • Never writes your world, your NPCs or your plot for you

How it works

Nothing new to learn at the table.

  1. Write as usual

    Each player types or uploads their notes after the session. No templates, no tags.

  2. Tablescribe connects it

    Entries are filed, notes are merged, and open threads get their new clues.

  3. Everyone catches up

    Players read their recap. You see what has gone quiet and plan the next session your way.
