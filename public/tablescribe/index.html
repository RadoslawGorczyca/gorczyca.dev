What it does

Prep less. Remember everything.

Automatic filing

Write one messy note per session. Tablescribe creates or updates a page for every NPC, location and item it finds, with links back to the sessions where they appeared.

A shared campaign vault

Invite your players. Everyone sees the public campaign record, and each player keeps private notes and backstory only you and they can read.

Investigation board AI

Ask "What is the cult actually planning?" and get a theory built only from your campaign notes, with the clues it used. Players can run their own theories in private.

See what your players suspect AI

Read your players' theories in one place, so you can lean into the plotlines they are most excited about.

Rules lookups in context

Ask how a situation from tonight's session works under the rules, answered from rulebooks you upload or from the free SRD.

Notes in, notes out

Write in Tablescribe or upload the note files you already have. Your players can upload theirs, and everything lands in one campaign.

The party scribe

One player writes. The whole table remembers.

Many tables hand the notebook to one player, for a session, an arc or the whole campaign. Tablescribe gives that player a proper job: a quick way to jot during play, an AI draft to tidy up afterwards, and a clean recap the rest of the table can build on.

  1. Session 12Mira
  2. Session 13Mira
  3. Session 14 · nowBram
  4. Session 15Bram
  5. Session 16 · nextIlse

Pick one scribe for the campaign, or rotate every few sessions. Everyone gets a reminder before their turn.

  1. Jot during play

    Fast capture with timestamps. Type @Vessa or #Saltmere and it links to existing entries.

  2. Draft in minutes

    After the session the AI turns the jots into a readable recap and proposes new or updated entries.

  3. Publish to the table

    The scribe reviews and publishes. The GM can turn on a quick check before anything goes out.

  4. Margin notes

    Other players suggest corrections and additions. The scribe accepts them, and the log keeps credit for every session written.

How it works

Three minutes after the session.

  1. Dump the session

    You or this week's scribe paste or type notes the way you always do. No templates, no tagging.

  2. Review what was filed

    Check the new and updated entries, fix anything the scribe got wrong, and approve.

  3. Your table catches up

    Players read the recap, update their private notes, and start theorising before next week.
