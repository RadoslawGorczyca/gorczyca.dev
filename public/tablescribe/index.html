Everyone's notes, one campaign

Five notebooks. One story.

Each player writes or uploads their own notes. Tablescribe merges them into one campaign record and keeps a page for every NPC, location and item, with links back to the session where it showed up.

The stand-in scribe

Missed a session? Someone took notes for you.

When a player can't make it, another one takes the notebook for the night. Their notes become a recap the missing player can read before next week.

How it works

Nothing new to learn at the table.

  1. Write as usual

    Each player types or uploads their notes after the session. No templates, no tags.

  2. Tablescribe connects it

    Entries are filed, notes are merged, and open threads get their new clues.

  3. You steer the story

    Check what your table is chasing and plan next session around it. Rules questions get answers from rulebooks you upload or the free SRD.
